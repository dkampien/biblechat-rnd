Core palette

The brand ink is a purple-navy, not black. Green carries growth and action, gold carries warmth and reward, coral appears only inside gradients and moments of emotion.

06 · Voice

Warm, plain-spoken, unhurried

Second person, present tense. Short declaratives for empathy, verb-first bullets for benefits. Spiritual, never preachy.

Have you made time for God's Word today?

App Store description, opening hook

No ads. No pressure. Just God's Word, made accessible.

Website

Walk with God, one day at a time.

Release notes, signature closer
  • Empathy first: "Life is noisy. Fast. Overwhelming." Then the app as the calm answer.
  • Benefit bullets start with a verb: Understand the Bible Better. Grow Your Prayer Habit. Make Faith Part of Your Day.
  • CTAs are gentle: "Get started", never urgency or guilt.
  • Never use em dashes in brand copy.

07 · Imagery and motion

Iridescent scripture, watercolor calm

Hero illustration

Flat minimalist iridescent biblical scenes. The house generation prompt survives verbatim in the asset filenames:

"flat minimalist iridescent happenings from the bible"

Watercolor landscapes

Meditations and store screenshots use soft watercolor paintings: forest paths, sunrises, meadows, olive trees, framed like paintings on paper.

Line icons and mascot

Flat single-color icon set (icBible, icLeaf, prayerHands, stars). One vector mascot, "Sam". The cross mark exists as SVG cut-out and full sunburst icon.

Celebration motion

Lottie sparkles, stars, confetti, trophies and gifts mark reward moments: purchases, streaks, completed journeys.
