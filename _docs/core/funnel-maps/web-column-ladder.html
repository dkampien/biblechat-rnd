Reading the ladder

Every cost-per is one spend pool. Cost-per at a rung = cost-per at the rung above ÷ the rate in. The biggest seam is registrations per landing page view, 27%: it alone turns a $1-2 page view into a $5 email.
Results change meaning per ad set. A registration-optimized row shows cost per result near $5; a purchase-optimized row near $63. Both are "Cost per result". Read the fine print before the number.
The signature inverts the app row. Landing page views and registrations fill; App installs and Cost per app install stay blank. A web row with installs is misconfigured, not successful.
Purchase counts trials too. The pixel fires Purchase on the $6.49 one-week trial as well as the $18.99 intro. Splitting them is a Stripe or PostHog read, not a column.
Two registrations per buyer. CompleteRegistration fires at email capture and again on the download click after payment, so registrations run up to 8% high on rows with many buyers.
The ladder ends at the paid intro. Renewal at $42.20 is a cohort question. Keep the tripwire rung visible so a cheap CPA and a bad cohort cannot coexist unnoticed.
