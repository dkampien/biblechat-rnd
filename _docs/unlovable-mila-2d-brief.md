# unlovable-mila-2d — creative brief

**Concept:** unlovable-after-betrayal · impossible-to-love · daily-plan · angle: love-never-left
**Form:** duo-dialogue-drama × crisis-arc · video · long · delivery: dialogue

## The world

**Treatment:** Hand-drawn 2D cel animation in the style of an early-2000s theatrical family film: soft watercolor-painted backgrounds, clean subtle linework, rounded shapes, large expressive eyes, flat colors with soft shading, warm gentle palette.

**Set:** A storybook meadow: a still pond in the foreground with clear reflections, a field of tulips, a windmill on the left, snowy mountains in the distance.

**Music:** Instrumental only, no vocals: sparse felt piano, slow and minor, growing gently into warm strings, ending resolved and hopeful.

## Cast

- **Jesus** (never speaks): Jesus as a dignified children's-Bible illustration: a simple robe, warm gentle presence, face softly indistinct or seen from behind or at three-quarter distance. Reverent, never comedic.
- **the girl** (voice: Lizy - Sweet, Charming and Captivating): A human girl of six or seven: round face, large brown eyes, warm brown skin, long black hair in two low braids, a yellow sundress with small white daisies, barefoot. Calm, matter-of-fact, warm.
- **Mila** (voice: Mandy - Warm, Soft and Pleasant): A small alien-like creature about toddler height: soft rounded body, oversized head, large expressive dark eyes with visible light reflections, longer lashes and a soft female-coded face, muted dusty-periwinkle faded-denim-blue coloring with a lighter cream-blue belly patch, short rounded slightly drooping teddy-bear-like ears with a small healed nick in one ear, soft four-fingered paws, a small stitched patch on one arm, a few faded scuffs, slightly tired eyes. Loved-once and worn, clearly alive, never a literal toy.

## Script

### WOUND

**Mila:** "Oh God… when I look at my reflection, I see someone impossible to love."
> Wide establishing at golden hour: Mila stands small at the pond's edge, hunched, arms wrapped around herself, staring at her reflection. Slow gentle push-in. Her mouth does not move; the line is inner voice.

**the girl:** "Why would you say that?" *(same take as previous)*
> The girl enters quietly and sits down beside Mila at the pond, no greeting. Two-shot from slightly behind, both reflections visible in the water.

**Mila:** "Because of what I did. I broke a heart that trusted me. Someone like me doesn't get loved again."
> Cut closer on Mila for the confession, eyes down at the water.

**Mila:** "Not even by God." *(same take as previous)*
> Close on Mila's face, eyes down at the water. The emotional floor, quieter than everything before it.

### STORY

**the girl:** "Can I tell you about a woman Jesus met at a well?"
> Soft close-up on the girl. She looks at the pond, then at Mila, and offers the story, unhurried and kind.

**the girl:** "Everyone in her town knew what she'd done. She came to the water alone, so nobody would look at her."
> Storybook interlude begins: parchment watercolor, a stone well in dry noon heat, a woman walking alone with a water jar, villagers distant and turned away.

**the girl:** "And Jesus was there. He knew everything — every single thing she did. And he didn't leave. He offered her living water… so she would never feel empty again."
> Storybook: Jesus at the well with the woman, seen at three-quarter distance, face softly indistinct, dignified. He speaks with her; light gathers on the well's water.

**the girl:** "She was the one everyone gave up on. She's in the Bible… because he loved her anyway."
> Storybook: close on the woman's face changing, wonder replacing shame, warm light blooming on the well's water. The glowing water fills the frame at the end, setting up the match-cut back to the pond.

### OBJECTION

**Mila:** "But how? How do I feel loved like that again?"
> Back at the pond, the match-cut landing on its water. Close on Mila, eyes wet, hope trying to break through the doubt.

### THESIS

**the girl:** "God's love never left you. But feeling it again… that part is like any love. You have to show up every day. Small things. And He meets you there."
> The payload shot: the girl frontal, medium close-up, angled slightly toward camera so the viewer feels addressed without breaking the scene. Simple certainty, never preaching.

### OBJECTION

**Mila:** "Every day? I don't even know where to start."
> Two-shot: Mila turns to the girl, a little desperate, anxiety pace. The real objection is overwhelm, not disbelief.

### PRODUCT

**Mila:** "…You're giving me an app?" *(same take as previous)*
> Same take: the girl holds out the phone, screen a soft warm indistinct glow, never readable UI. Mila looks at the phone, then at her, genuinely puzzled; gentle visual comedy without breaking the warm tone.

**the girl:** "It's a Bible app. You tell it what you're carrying — it gives you a plan. Ten minutes a day, made just for you."
> Close on the girl still offering the phone, a small smile audible, the screen only a soft warm glow.

### OBJECTION

**Mila:** "That's… it?"
> Close on Mila, quiet disbelief that it could be this simple, eyes on the offered phone.

### THESIS

**the girl:** "Why would it have to be harder? God doesn't want to watch you struggle."
> Close on the girl: the child's plain certainty. The objection-killer works because a child says it simply; zero salesmanship.

### DECISION

**Mila:** "Then I'll try. Thank you."
> Two-shot: Mila takes the phone gently, looks at it, then at the girl. The first light breath in the whole video; a soft beat between the two sentences.

### AFTER

> Next morning, soft pink-gold light, same pond. Mila sits comfortably with the phone mid-use, screen a soft glow only. She looks up, catches her reflection in the pond, and this time smiles at it, posture open. The girl waves from near the windmill in the background. She returns to the phone, hope readable on her face; hold; slow fade to black. No voiceover, no end card; music and image only.
