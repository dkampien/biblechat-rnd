Two routes · six bands · three doors · structure from the funnel config, the builder, the App Store and Ads Manager · no numbers: those live in the ladders

BibleChat funnel map

Bands are depths: what the person has committed so far. Doors at the top are the campaign families; each aims Meta's delivery at one checkpoint. The two routes are threads through the bands, each in its own order. Every box names a checkpoint, the sensor that counts it, and how finely it can be read: per ad, per monthly cohort, or not read yet. The numbers per route live in the web column ladder and the app column ladder.

web route app route where a door aims money changes hands no sensor reads it yet

Where the two routes differ

  • Web pays in band 5 before it installs in band 3; the app installs first.
  • Web personalizes in band 2 (the quiz); the app personalizes in band 4 (onboarding).
  • Web money is a paid intro on Stripe; app money is a store trial that converts, or a direct purchase, or a web checkout by rule.
  • Web is read per ad down to the purchase; the app is read per ad down to the trial. Below that, both are read per monthly cohort.

Instances under each route

  • Web: 5 quiz funnels (christian-archetype carries about 75% of spend). Same checkpoints, different rates.
  • App: 66 custom product pages (64 live) times about 8 onboardings selectable by a deeplink intent on the ad. Which ad pairs with which page is not readable yet.
  • The map is the same for every instance; only the rates in the ladders change.
