Reading an ad without fooling yourself

Compare only inside one door. A CPT row and a CPI row differ in optimization event and window; their CPCs and CPIs are not comparable.
Landing page views are not a leak here. The click lands on Apple's page, where no pixel lives. Blank means no sensor, not no people.
100 impressions before reading anything; 200 to compare two ads. Below that the rates are noise.
A cheap CPT can be a bad CPT. If the ad pulls low-intent trials, the cohort's trial-to-paid falls and nothing per ad shows it. Watch trial-to-paid by channel as the tripwire.
Never buy a trial above $22 gross (that is 100% projected month-12 return); $16 is cash break-even after the store cut. Say which rule a ceiling is built on.
Starvation is not a verdict. An ad Meta never delivered has no read; rerun it, do not kill it.
