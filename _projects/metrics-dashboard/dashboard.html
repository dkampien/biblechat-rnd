Gross and cash
Gross is what customers paid. Cash (proceeds) is what remains after Apple and Google commission, about 74% of gross blended. Net cash break-even therefore needs a gross return well above 100%.
Committed floor
Active subscriptions whose auto-renew is still on. Subscriptions that are cancelled but still inside their paid period are counted in the total, not in the floor.
ARR
MRR times 12, a snapshot of the current base, not a forecast.
Scope
The BibleChat app only (the kids app is a separate app id). Web and Stripe revenue is counted only where it flows through Adapty.
